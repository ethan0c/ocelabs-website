Project Questionnaire and How We Work


Part 1: Project questionnaire

Short answers are fine. Skip anything that does not apply. You fill it in online, at the link in our recap email. Anything we covered on the call is already filled in for you to check.


About you

1. What does your business do? One or two sentences is plenty.

2. Who are your customers, and how do they usually find you?

3. What do you want people to do after visiting the site? For example: call you, book an appointment, buy something, or fill in a form.


The site

4. Do you have a website now? If so, what do you like about it, what bugs you, and roughly how many pages or blog posts does it have?

5. Which pages do you want? Most sites have Home, About, Services and Contact. Add anything else you have in mind.

6. Is the text for the site written yet, or will you need help with it?

7. Do you have a logo, brand colours or photos we should use?

8. Name two or three websites you like and what you like about them. They can be from any industry.


Being found

9. What would someone type into Google to find a business like yours?

10. Where are your customers? A town, a region, the whole country?

11. Do you have a Google Business listing (the box that shows up on Google Maps) or a mailing list we should keep in mind?


Practical

12. Do you already own a web address (like yourbusiness.com)? Which one, and who did you buy it through?

13. Does the site need to do anything beyond showing information? For example: take bookings, take payments, sell products, sell tickets, or have a members-only area. If you already use a tool for any of this (like Square, Calendly or Shopify), tell us which. If you sell products, roughly how many?

14. For those features, is a well-known tool fine (booking through Calendly, a store on Shopify), or does it need to work in a way no existing tool does? If so, describe how.

15. Who has the final say on the site?

16. After launch, who will keep the site up to date, and how often do you expect changes?

17. Is there a date you need the site live by? If you have a budget in mind, let us know that too.


Before we start

The project begins, and the timeline starts counting, on the kickoff day. Kickoff happens when all four of these are in:

• The deposit is paid (usually 50% upfront, but larger projects may have a different payment structure)
• This questionnaire is returned
• Your logo, brand files, and any photography you want used are shared with us
• Any existing copy you want kept is shared with us

You do not need finished copy to start. We write a first draft of every page from your questionnaire answers, and you edit it. If you would rather we handle the writing completely, choose the Copywriting option.

Anything still missing one week after kickoff moves the launch date by the same number of days. If an asset is still missing at launch, the site goes live with a placeholder and we swap it in when it arrives.



Part 2: How we work


Search and AI: built in, not bolted on

Most search optimisation is fixing a site after it was built wrong. We build it right the first time.

Every page is delivered as complete, readable HTML before any script runs. Google, Bing, and AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude read the same clean page a person does. Nothing important is hidden behind loading screens or generated only in the browser.

Each page has one clear title, one honest description, and headings that say what each section is about in plain language. That is what search engines rank and what AI models quote when they answer a question about your industry. We add structured data so your business name, location, hours, and services are machine-readable. We add a sitemap so every page is found, and a preview image so your links look right when shared on social media or in messages.

We think of this as being legible: to people first, and to machines as a consequence. There are no tricks, and nothing that needs to be redone when the next algorithm change arrives.


Our tools, and why we do not use monthly-fee platforms

We build with Next.js, the most widely used framework for modern websites, and host on Vercel, the company that maintains it. The code is written by us, specifically for you, and stored in a private repository that you can be given access to at any time.

Platforms like Squarespace, Wix, and Webflow charge a monthly fee for as long as your site exists. In exchange you get templates that look like everyone else's, pages that load slower than they should, limited control over search settings, and a site you cannot take with you if you ever leave.

Our approach costs more up front and almost nothing after. There is no platform subscription. Hosting at typical traffic is free or a few dollars a month. The domain name is the one fixed annual cost, and it is registered in your name. You own the site outright, including the code.


Hosting and security

Fast everywhere. Pages are generated ahead of time and served from a global network of servers, so a visitor in Lagos and a visitor in New York both receive the page in well under a second.

Secure by default. Every site runs over HTTPS with a certificate that renews itself automatically. Browsers are instructed never to load the site insecurely.

No server to break into. There is no database or admin login sitting on the open internet. Contact forms are handled by a dedicated form service, so your inbox receives messages without the site ever storing them.

Nothing to patch. Because there is no plugin system, there are no plugin updates to forget and no vulnerabilities to inherit from someone else's code.

Always up. The hosting network has no single machine that can fail. Each deployment goes live in one step or not at all, and any previous version of the site can be restored in seconds.
