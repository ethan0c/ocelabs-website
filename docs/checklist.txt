PART 2: PER PROJECT

Copy this block into the tracker row. Every line is one action and one button.

Inquiry
[ ] Reply within 1 business day with Email A and the Cal.com link
[ ] Add the tracker row; Stage: New; Next action: "Nudge" in 3 business days
[ ] No booking after 3 days: Email A2. No reply 7 days later: Closed lost

Intro call
[ ] 10 min before: reread their message, open their site, look at two competitors, pick a likely package
[ ] On the call: say the package and its range out loud. No fixed price
[ ] Stage: Call booked

Recap
[ ] Same day: Email B with the questionnaire (client-onboarding doc) attached
[ ] Stage: Recap sent; Next action: nudge in 5 business days

Proposal (within 2 business days of the questionnaire)
[ ] /pricing: build the quote from the questionnaire answers
[ ] Fill in Client (legal business name) and Client email
[ ] Press "Download proposal PDF"
[ ] Upload the PDF to the e-sign tool, send for signature
[ ] Email C with the same PDF attached
[ ] Paste the /pricing link into the tracker's Quote column
[ ] Stage: Proposal sent; Next action: nudge day 5, again day 12; expires day 14

Signature and deposit (same day the signed copy arrives)
[ ] Open the quote from the tracker's Quote link
[ ] Press "Draft in Stripe" on the Deposit line
[ ] Open in Stripe, read it over, press Send
[ ] Email D
[ ] Create the client's shared folder; put the signed proposal in 02
[ ] Stage: Signed; Next action: chase the four kickoff items

Kickoff (when all four are in: deposit cleared, questionnaire, brand files, copy)
[ ] Email E with kickoff date, launch date, content deadline (kickoff + 7 days), review dates
[ ] Private repository, staging link
[ ] Stage: Kickoff → Building

Build
[ ] Every Friday: Email F (done, next, needed from you), even if nothing is needed
[ ] Design review: one round of feedback per email, two rounds included
[ ]   Web App only: press "Draft in Stripe" on the Design approved line when they approve; send it
[ ] Staging review: same
[ ] Any extra: written change order, "approved" in writing, added to the final invoice
[ ] Stage: Review when staging goes out

Launch (client approves staging in writing)
[ ] Press "Draft in Stripe" on the Balance line; add approved change orders as extra lines; send
[ ] Email G
[ ] Paid → connect domain, go live
[ ] Stage: Launched

Handover (site is live)
[ ] Email H: live URL, repository access, hosting access, Search Console, content guide
[ ] Put the handover files in 05
[ ] Tracker: Next action date = today + 30

Day 30
[ ] Email I: one observation from analytics, offer the retainer, ask for a testimonial and a referral
[ ] Retainer yes → Stripe subscription, not a manual invoice
[ ] Add the project to /work if they agree
[ ] Stage: Closed (won)
