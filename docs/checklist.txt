PART 2: PER PROJECT

The lead page shows the buttons for the stage it is in. This is the same list, for reading ahead.

Inquiry
[ ] Automatic: lead created, Email A sent with the Cal.com link, inbox pinged
[ ] Reply personally in the thread if their message calls for it
[ ] Automatic: no booking after 3 business days → Email A2; 7 more days of silence → Closed lost

Intro call
[ ] 10 min before: reread their message, open their site, look at two competitors, pick a likely package
[ ] On the call: say the package and its range out loud. No fixed price
[ ] Automatic: a Cal.com booking sets Call booked (or press Set call time on the lead)

Recap
[ ] Same day: on the lead, paste the call notes → Draft the recap → check it → Send recap (Email B goes with the questionnaire link)
[ ] Automatic: questionnaire answers land on the lead; one nudge after 5 business days

Proposal (within 2 business days of the questionnaire)
[ ] Lead → Build quote (estimator opens for this lead) → Save to lead
[ ] Lead → Send proposal (Email C with the signing link)
[ ] Automatic: nudges day 5 and 12; expiry day 14

Signature and deposit
[ ] Automatic when they sign: signed PDF + Email D, deposit invoice sent from Stripe, schedule recorded
[ ] Create the client's shared folder; signed PDF (from the lead page) in 02

Kickoff
[ ] Mark brand files and copy received on the lead as they arrive (deposit and questionnaire tick themselves)
[ ] Automatic when the fourth item is in: Email E with kickoff, launch, content deadline, review dates
[ ] Private repository, staging link

Build
[ ] Fridays: finish the drafted Email F in Gmail and send it, even if nothing is needed
[ ] Design review: one round of feedback per email, two rounds included
[ ] Web App only: Design approved on the lead when signed off (sends the 30% invoice)
[ ] Any extra: written change order, "approved" in writing; enter it when approving staging

Launch (client approves staging in writing)
[ ] Lead → Staging approved, with change orders (final invoice + Email G go out)
[ ] Paid → connect domain, go live

Handover (site is live)
[ ] Lead → Live, with the domain and the handover links (Email H goes out; day 30 is set)
[ ] Handover files in 05

Day 30
[ ] Automatic: Email I drafted into Gmail on the day; add the one real observation and send
[ ] Retainer yes → Stripe subscription, not a manual invoice
[ ] Add the project to /work if they agree
